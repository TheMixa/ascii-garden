# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

ASCII Garden ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

| Permission | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| `bookmarks` | Reads your Bookmarks Bar and Other Bookmarks folders so they can be shown on the new tab page. Bookmarks are never changed and never leave your browser. |
| `favicon` | Shows each bookmark's site icon from the browser's own icon cache. |
| `search` | Sends what you type in the search box to **your browser's default search engine**, exactly as if you had typed it in the address bar. |

## What is stored

Your settings (name, number of animals, trails on or off, bookmarks bar on or off, chosen animal) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not synced or sent anywhere by the extension.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, no ads and no remote code.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at <https://github.com/TheMixa/ascii-garden/issues>.
